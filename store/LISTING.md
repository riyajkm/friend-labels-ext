# Chrome Web Store listing (copy/paste)

**Name:** Friend Labels
**Category:** Productivity
**Summary (max 132 chars):** Labels your Facebook friends Keep, Review or Remove and shows a local dashboard. Display only, nothing leaves your browser.

**Description:**
Friend Labels helps you tidy a long friends list without guessing.

• Adds a Keep / Review / Remove label, a reason and a 0-100 score to every card on your Friends page
• Notices which friends react or comment on your posts and raises their score
• Star friends to protect them
• Dashboard with charts, search, filters, notes and CSV export
• Adjustable rules

Display only: it never unfriends, likes, messages or sends requests. You decide everything.
Private: all data stays in your browser's local storage. No servers, no analytics.
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta or Facebook.

**Single purpose:** Show analysis labels for the user's Facebook friends list.
**Permission justifications:**
- storage / unlimitedStorage: save labels, settings, protected list and notes locally.
- Host access to facebook.com: read friend cards and the user's own posts' reactions to compute labels.
**Remote code:** No.
