Friend Labels – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Friend Labels is a browser extension that runs entirely on your device. Short version: your data never leaves your browser.

What it reads

On facebook.com pages you open, it reads friend names, profile links, mutual-friend counts, whether a profile photo is set, and, on your own posts, which friends reacted or commented.

Where it is stored

Only in chrome.storage.local on your device, together with your rules, protected list and notes.

What it does not do

  • No data is sent to any server, including ours.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • It never unfriends, likes, messages or sends requests. It only displays labels.

Your control

Export your data to CSV any time. "Clear all data" in the dashboard, or uninstalling the extension, deletes everything.

Permissions

storage and unlimitedStorage save the above locally. Access to www.facebook.com and web.facebook.com lets it show labels on the page.

Not affiliated with Meta

Friend Labels is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. or Facebook.
