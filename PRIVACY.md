# Privacy Policy – Friend Labels

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Friend Labels runs entirely in your browser.

**What it reads:** on facebook.com pages you open, it reads the friend names, profile links, mutual-friend counts, photo presence, and (on your own posts) who reacted or commented.

**What it stores:** that data, plus your settings, protected list, notes and statuses, in `chrome.storage.local` on your device only.

**What it does not do:** it does not send data to any server, use analytics or trackers, sell or share data, or run remote code.

**Your control:** export to CSV any time; "Clear all data" in the dashboard (or removing the extension) deletes everything.

**Permissions:** `storage` / `unlimitedStorage` to save the above locally; host access to `facebook.com` to show labels on the page.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/riyajkm/friend-labels/issues
