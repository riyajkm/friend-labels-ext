      Rules

      Deactivated accounts are always Remove. Anyone who reacts to or comments on your posts is always Keep. Starred friends are always Keep.

      How the score works

      • Mutual friends up to 55 points. More mutual friends means a real connection.
      • Profile photo 15 points. The grey default picture usually means an abandoned account.
      • Engagement up to 30 points. Open the reactions list or comments on your own posts and Friend Labels notes who interacted. Use “Scan all reactions” inside a reactions list to read the whole list.
      • Removed is detected when you unfriend someone yourself and their card changes to “Add friend”.
