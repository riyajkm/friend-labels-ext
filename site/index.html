Safe by design

  • No auto-unfriend. It never clicks Unfriend for you.
  • No auto-like, no bulk friend requests, no messages. Display only.
  • 100% local. Data stays in your browser. No servers, no analytics.
  • Open source (MIT). Read every line.

What you get

  • A label, reason and 0–100 score on every friend card
  • Friends who react or comment on your posts score higher
  • Star friends to always keep them
  • Dashboard with charts, search, filters, notes and CSV export
  • Rules you can adjust, plus a daily removal counter

Built for big lists
